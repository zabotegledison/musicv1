Map each rhythm onto scales, pentatonics, arpeggios, intervals or your own musical vocabulary, then explore it over a selected Brazilian backing track and a chord or modal centre.

Study Length is fixed at 2 bars for focused fragment practice.
The study is always in 2/4. Each fragment lasts 1/4: the app always uses 2 fragments per bar.
Generate continuously changing combinations and use them to develop listening, reading, recognition and rhythmic flexibility.
Explore the micro-cells one at a time. Learn to recognize their sound, notation and behaviour in relation to the pulse.
Explore patterns drawn from different Brazilian musical contexts and observe how the micro-units organize themselves within larger structures.
Move from one pattern to another while maintaining pulse, continuity and phrasing.
Choose 2 to 8 patterns. If the progression is shorter than the study length, it will repeat.
The app will randomly choose enough traditional patterns to fill the selected study length.
Alternate recognizable patterns with generated material, relating contextualized structures to less predictable combinations.
Let the system automatically choose between the different possibilities available in Hybrid Pattern. Use it as a transition towards freer improvisation. Move beyond the written pattern and improvise freely. This step chooses everything automatically: pattern opening or pattern ending, free random or A/B color-matched random. No extra choices.
Block size is fixed at 2 bars: 4 fragments. Opening/ending forms replace one bar inside the pattern phrase.
Choose the Brazilian rhythmic context in which to practise the material.
Practice the material over different Brazilian rhythmic environments. Backing loops are pre-rendered at several tempos, keeping pitch clean at any Study BPM. If no track is selected, a simple metronome plays instead.
