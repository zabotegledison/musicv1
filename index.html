Brazilian Syncopation Lab

A pedagogical method for developing a Brazilian-inspired rhythmic vocabulary for improvisation and musical creation.

Study Length is fixed at 2 bars for focused fragment practice.
The study is always in 2/4. Each fragment lasts 1/4: the app always uses 2 fragments per bar.
Hear fragments combined freely, with no fixed pattern. This builds raw exposure to the full rhythmic vocabulary before any structure is introduced.
Get to know two fragments up close. Starts with A1 (tension) and B1 (resolution), but you can freely combine any of the 15 fragments in either position. This builds the very first two-bar phrase of the method.
Explore a collection of traditional patterns and fragments inspired by classic melodies and rhythmic practices of Brazilian music. Study one or two patterns at a time before moving on to continuous rotation and hybrid combinations.
Study the transition from one pattern to another without stopping.
Choose 2 to 8 patterns. If the progression is shorter than the study length, it will repeat.
The app will randomly choose enough traditional patterns to fill the selected study length.
Mix 2-bar traditional-pattern blocks with 2-bar random-fragment blocks. This tests how free fragments work inside a traditional rhythmic grammar.
This step chooses everything automatically: pattern opening or pattern ending, free random or A/B color-matched random. No extra choices.
Block size is fixed at 2 bars: 4 fragments. Opening/ending forms replace one bar inside the pattern phrase.
Backing loops are pre-rendered at several tempos, keeping pitch clean at any Study BPM. If no track is selected, a simple metronome plays instead.
